The largest single drop-off occurs between Product View and Add to Cart — only 22.1% of product viewers proceed. Every other transition retains at least 61%. For BMW, this maps to the gap between the model page and configurator entry — the single highest-priority optimisation point.

02 / 03 — Return visitor lift

1st 0.48%, 2nd 2.01%, 3rd-5th 3.99%, 6th+ 24.94%.
Conversion grows monotonically with every return visit. Users on their 6th+ visit convert at 51.5× the rate of first-time visitors. A 24-hour retargeting window discards most eventual buyers. Switch to 5–7 days. User-level analysis. High-session bands reflect self-selected high-intent users.

03 / 03 — Device × channel gap

Desktop: referral 1.70%, organic 1.09%, cpc 1.09%. Mobile: referral 1.76%, organic 1.23%, cpc 0.97%.
The mobile gap is channel-specific, not universal. Mobile referral already outperforms desktop. The CPC gap — mobile 0.97% vs desktop 1.09% — is where the problem lives. Fix paid landing pages, not the entire mobile experience.

What this means for BMW Portugal

Positions taken
01

BMW's highest-demand model has no online purchase path

The iX3 is +750% and BREAKOUT in Portuguese web search (ix3 bmw 2026, bmw ix3 neue klasse — Google Trends PT, Web + YouTube, 12 June 2026). The i4 offers Reserve — a 4-step online checkout. The Reserve CTA gap appears at Step 14 of 14 — after completing the full iX3 configurator. The i4 presents Reservar at Step 12 of 12. A buyer must complete a 14-step, €64,500 configuration to discover there is no digital way to commit. Observed bmw.pt, desktop + mobile, 12 June 2026. This is a strategic sequencing decision confirmed in BMW Group's 2024 Annual Report: MINI brand direct sales launched across ten European markets in 2024; BMW brand direct sales in Europe is planned but not yet live. The digital funnel needs to be ready when it does.

What the data shows

One transition drives most of the loss

A 77.9% drop between product view and the next step accounts for more funnel loss than all other stages combined. For BMW, this maps to model page to configurator entry. Optimising this single transition produces more incremental leads than any downstream improvement.

Re-engagement windows are too short

Cox Automotive 2024 Car Buyer Journey Study (n=2,310, published January 2025): new-vehicle buying requires 13.5+ hours of active research across an average of 4.1 websites. BEV buyers are more digitally engaged than ICE buyers throughout. A standard 24-hour retargeting window discards the majority of eventual buyers. This is a campaign settings change — not a creative problem — and costs nothing to test.

Fix CPC mobile landing pages, not mobile broadly

Mobile referral already outperforms desktop. The CPC gap is where the problem lives. The configurator performs identically on mobile (observed June 2026). The friction is upstream, in the paid landing experience before the configurator is reached.

02

BMW leads search interest — but Mercedes is capturing used car demand

BMW averages 83 vs Mercedes 76 in Portuguese web search (Google Trends, past year). But Mercedes's rising queries include "mercedes usados certificados" (+110%). BMW's rising queries are entirely new-car focused. Mercedes is capturing demand at two funnel points; BMW at one.

03

BMW is moving toward direct digital sales — but not there yet

The BMW Group 2024 Annual Report established a dedicated "Customer Channels, Digital Commerce, Connected Company" unit and confirmed MINI's transition to direct sales in Europe in 2025, with the BMW brand direct sales introduction referenced as forthcoming. BMW delivered 426,536 BEVs in 2024 — a 13.5% increase, with BEVs accounting for 17.4% of total deliveries. The infrastructure for capturing iX3 demand digitally is being built. The iX3 Reserve gap is a transition problem, not a structural one.

Priority recommendation

What changes with real BMW GA4 access

Every position above is directionally valid but inferred from proxy data and public signals. With access to BMW's analytics backend: the exact configurator step where mobile exits on paid traffic, the actual session count for iX3 configurator users, the true re-engagement window for configurator abandoners, and BEV vs ICE completion rates by model. BMW is already collecting this data. Running these queries takes hours.

Three testable product hypotheses

Sprint-sized decisions, falsifiable success metrics
H1

Measure iX3 configurator entry rate vs i4

The iX3 has BREAKOUT status in Portuguese web search — 'ix3 bmw 2026' and 'bmw ix3 neue klasse' are top rising queries. The open question is whether that demand is entering the 14-step configurator or dissipating on the model page. Segment configurator_start rate by model, isolating iX3 vs i4. If iX3 entry rate is lower than i4 despite higher search interest, the model page CTA — not the configurator — is the fix. Success metric: configurator entry rate for iX3 vs i4, by device and channel. Cost: one BigQuery query against BMW's GA4 export.

H2

An online reservation on the iX3 will capture intent that currently expires at dealer hours

The i4 has a 4-step Reserve flow. The iX3 does not. Cox Automotive 2024 Car Buyer Journey Study (n=2,310, published January 2025): new-vehicle buying requires 13.5+ hours of active research across an average of 4.1 websites. BEV buyers are more digitally engaged than ICE buyers throughout. Most of that research time falls outside dealer hours. Add the same Reserve CTA to the iX3 configurator summary screen. Success metric: lead submission rate for iX3 vs i4 within 14 days of configurator completion. Secondary: reservation conversion rate by time of day. Cost: one sprint to extend the existing Reserve flow to the iX3.

H3

A dedicated mobile landing page for iX3 CPC traffic will close the paid mobile gap

Mobile CPC converts at 0.97% vs desktop 1.09%. iX3 is the highest-demand model in Portugal. A dedicated mobile landing page for iX3 paid search campaigns, loading the configurator directly at Step 1 with the iX3 pre-selected, bypasses the model range page and reduces drop-off before configurator entry. Surface a lead form as an alternative CTA for users not ready to configure. Success metric: CPC conversion rate on mobile for iX3. Baseline: 0.97%. Target: parity with desktop CPC (1.09%) within 60 days. Cost: one sprint. No configurator changes required.
