The largest single drop-off occurs between Product View and Add to Cart — only 19.7% of product viewers proceed. Every other transition retains at least 61%. For BMW, this maps to the gap between the model page and configurator entry — the single highest-priority optimisation point.

02 / 03 — Return visitor lift

1st 0.78%, 2nd 2.01%, 3rd-5th 3.99%, 6th+ 4.94%.
Conversion grows monotonically with every return visit. Users on their 6th+ visit convert at 6.3× the rate of first-time visitors. A 24-hour retargeting window discards most eventual buyers. Switch to 5–7 days.

03 / 03 — Device × channel gap

Desktop: referral 7.01%, organic 4.86%, cpc 5.06%. Mobile: referral 7.53%, organic 5.44%, cpc 4.28%.
The mobile gap is channel-specific, not universal. Mobile referral outperforms desktop. The CPC gap — mobile 4.28% vs desktop 5.06% — is where the problem lives. Fix paid landing pages, not the entire mobile experience.

What this means for BMW Portugal

Positions taken
01

One transition drives most of the loss

An 80.3% drop between product view and the next step accounts for more funnel loss than all other stages combined. For BMW, this maps to model page to configurator entry. Optimising this single transition produces more incremental leads than any downstream improvement.

02

Re-engagement windows are too short

Converters take a median of 3 sessions and 129 hours from first visit to purchase. A standard 24-hour retargeting window discards the majority of eventual buyers. This is a campaign settings change — not a creative problem — and costs nothing to test.

03

Fix CPC mobile landing pages, not mobile broadly

Mobile referral already outperforms desktop. The CPC gap is where the problem lives. The configurator performs identically on mobile (observed June 2026). The friction is upstream, in the paid landing experience before the configurator is reached.

04

BMW's highest-demand model has no online purchase path

The iX3 is +1,050% in Portuguese web search and BREAKOUT on YouTube. The i4 offers Reserve — a 4-step online checkout. The iX3 does not. The only conversion paths for BMW's most searched model are dealer-mediated, with 14 configuration steps, a €64,500 entry price, and an €8,400 decision at Step 1. This is a strategic sequencing decision confirmed in BMW Group's 2024 Annual Report: MINI brand direct sales launched across ten European markets in 2024; BMW brand direct sales in Europe is planned but not yet live. The digital funnel needs to be ready when it does.

05

BMW leads search interest — but Mercedes is capturing used car demand

BMW averages 83 vs Mercedes 76 in Portuguese web search (Google Trends, past year). But Mercedes's rising queries include "mercedes usados certificados" (+110%). BMW's rising queries are entirely new-car focused. Mercedes is capturing demand at two funnel points; BMW at one.

Priority recommendation

What changes with real BMW GA4 access

Every position above is directionally valid but inferred from proxy data and public signals. With access to BMW's analytics backend: the exact configurator step where mobile exits on paid traffic, the actual session count for iX3 configurator users, the true re-engagement window for configurator abandoners, and BEV vs ICE completion rates by model. BMW is already collecting this data. Running these queries takes hours.

Three testable product hypotheses

Sprint-sized decisions, falsifiable success metrics
H1

Reduce the lead form from 7 to 4 mandatory fields

The "Pedido de Proposta" form requires 7 mandatory fields before submission. Industry research consistently shows conversion drops beyond 3–4 fields. Proposed change: make Concessionário optional and remove the Particular/Empresa selector from the mandatory path. Success metric: lead submissions per configurator completion, segmented by mobile CPC traffic specifically — where the conversion gap is largest. Cost: one sprint. No configurator changes required.

H2

Add a save prompt at Step 6 of the iX3 configurator

Conversion grows 6.3× from first to sixth session. The save mechanism is currently gated behind a BMW ID login wall at Step 14 — after most users have already abandoned. Proposed change: surface a lightweight "Save and continue later" prompt at Step 6, requiring only an email address. Re-engage at 48 hours and 5 days. Success metric: return visit rate for users who save at Step 6 vs those who don't. Secondary: lead submission rate for save-feature return visitors. Cost: one sprint for the prompt UI; email automation via existing CRM.

H3

Build a dedicated mobile landing page for iX3 paid search traffic

Mobile CPC converts at 4.28% vs desktop 5.06%. Mobile referral outperforms desktop (7.53% vs 7.01%). The configurator is functionally identical on both devices — the friction is upstream, in the paid landing experience. Proposed change: dedicated mobile landing page loading the iX3 configurator directly at Step 1, with the two variants and price as the primary decision frame. Success metric: CPC conversion rate on mobile. Baseline 4.28%. Target: parity with desktop CPC (5.06%) within 60 days. Cost: one sprint. No configurator or campaign changes required.
